# Home page study (2026-10-28)

**Brief (Kellan):** the home page is only the studio intro, centred with more whitespace, and a way through to the game's page. Keep the flat biophilic / solarpunk look. Strata's subtitle: "A calm 3D puzzle game", not "nonogram".

**Inspected** (WebKit, 1440×900 and iPhone 13; captures in the Strata repo, `shots/studio-refs/`):
- Simogo, Snowman, ustwo games, inkle, Dinosaur Polo Club, thatgamecompany, Lucas Pope and okidokico.

**What the pages do:**
- **Simogo:** one plain sentence about who they are, then the games as tiles; the tiles are the navigation. Lucas Pope does the same with a list: the name, then the games.
- **thatgamecompany:** one large statement sentence is the whole first screen.
- **Snowman, okidokico and Dinosaur Polo Club:** a game's key art leads. Studios with many games or one big hit use this.
- **ustwo, Dinosaur Polo Club and thatgamecompany:** cookie banners and many nav items crowd the first screen. That's the thing to avoid.

**Applied:**
- **Home:** the studio's three lines, centred and fitting one screen on a phone, followed by one tile (the icon, the name and "A calm 3D puzzle game") linking to `/strata/`. That's Simogo's "games are the way in", with one game.
- **No nav bar:** with a single game, a nav bar would be scaffolding.
- **`/strata/`:** all of the game's detail moved here: the play link, the description, screenshots and the Strata help and privacy links.

**Open:**
- Desktop border at a quarter size: vine once or repeated.
- Edge style: spray, rough or clean (previews in `shots/mythen-variants/`).
